The final degree project work was produced with the help of several AI tools including mainly SOTA large language models, these tools allowed me to enrich the content quality, improving visualization and overall performance. Nevertheless, LLM's contributions were previously reviewed and supervised before implementing them into the workflow, to ensure accuracy and validity of the project's needs and findings. This second appendix will consist of a transparent list of the AI tools used, stating what stage they were used in and with what purpose.

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Claude}: this first LLM was used with the aim of generating some of the visual representations available at the documentation. For instance, all the workflow charts of the document (figures \ref{fig:arch_clean}, \ref{fig:minimalist_workflow_cycle}) were generated by this tool. Even if I was able to generate these charts by myself, I had no previous experience designing chart flows in LaTeX, so I prompted to generate the graph given the stages and the steps. Secondly, the Gantt chart was also created by Claude (figure \ref{fig:gantt_diagram}). I wanted to optimize time, and designing the graph in Excel from scratch would take some time, reason why I decided to give the tasks of the planing and I received the blank Gantt, perfect to be filled. 
	
	\item \textbf{Gemini}: because of its multi modal reasoning capacities and deep mathematical understanding, Gemini was responsible for dealing with the more technical aspects of the work. When I needed to transform formulas into LaTeX format I asked it to return them, moreover the \textit{unprojection} (section \ref{sec:unprojection_procedure}) function logic was proposed and refined by Gemini too. It proposed the foundational optic physics formulas  and camera properties logic. 
	
	On the other hand, Gemini is able to reason multi modal data such as images or videos. When I felt stuck in the reinforcement learning trainings or the training results were not successful, I would upload a screenshot or little video of the policy in action to get back its suggestions on how to tweak the hyperparameters or the reward weights of the training. 
	
	Finally, as mentioned in the Isaac Sim environment creation stages there is a possibility of creating the scenarios by code snippets instead of manually crafting them. Once I had handcrafted some of the basics and needed alternatives in my scenarios, I would prompt the LLM to return code snippets to automatically create environments with certain agent position and orientation specifications.  
\end{itemize}
